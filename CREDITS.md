# Credits

## Creator

**Mithrim** was designed and developed by **Saelthorn Haworth**.

Programming, game design, procedural generation, UI/UX, artwork integration, and original systems were created for this project.

## Inspirations

Mithrim draws inspiration from many classic role-playing games and tabletop systems, including but not limited to:

- Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition
- Diablo II
- Darkest Dungeon
- Tales of Maj'Eyal (ToME)
- Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup
- Pathfinder
- Classic ASCII Roguelikes

These works inspired gameplay mechanics and design philosophies that helped shape Mithrim.
